# Code of conduct

## Our standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, as its code of conduct. The full text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: everyone taking part in Runnerly — in issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other project space — is expected to behave respectfully
and in good faith. Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning or exclusionary
conduct are not acceptable, and neither is publishing someone's private
information without their permission.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and whenever someone is representing the
project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to `ayoub@abablil.me`. Reports are handled
privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may edit,
remove, or reject contributions that do not meet them, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone whose behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.
